About the Spiel

Where the Next
Generation Competes

The OVCA Junior Superspiel is one of the most anticipated junior curling events in the world, bringing together the region's most talented young curlers for a weekend of elite competition, camaraderie, and unforgettable moments on the ice.

Hosted across the Nation's Capital, this year's event features both a Men's and Women's draw — giving junior athletes the stage they deserve to compete at their highest level.

With over $30,000 in combined prizes on the line, this isn't just a spiel. It's a statement.
